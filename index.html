Lagos Market 101

Street Survival Handbook
🚶‍♂️💨

Rule #1: Never Accept the First Price!

Opening quotes in Lagos are ALWAYS inflated by 30% to 100%. Haggling is expected culture!

💬
Make Counter-Offers: Type your price in the counter box (e.g. 10k, 12,000, 500) or use quick percentage buttons (-35%, Half Price).
⚡
Mind the Patience Meter: Audacious lowballs insult the seller and drain patience. If patience drops to 0%, you get kicked out with empty hands!
🏃‍♂️
The Lagos Walkout (Walk Away): If negotiations stall, click Walk Away. 75% of the time, the seller panics and screams "Customer, come back!", dropping their price to rock-bottom!
🔍⚠️

Tactical Moves & Consequences

You can use one tactical trick per stall. Use them with wisdom!

🍯
Sweet Talk: Flatter the seller ("Chairman!", "Fine Mama!"). Softens their mood and restores +22% to +35% patience.
🔍
Inspect Fault:
• On Replicas / Thrift: Spots loose threads or flaws, forcing a 30% price reduction!
• ⚠️ On Certified Originals: BACKFIRES! Disrespecting genuine original material deeply insults the seller, triggers an immediate PRICE HIKE, and slashes -25% patience!
📞
Fake Call: Pretend a front shop is selling cheaper. Panic forces an immediate undercut!
💵
Show Cash: Slap raw cash on the stall table. Instant deal close if close to floor price!
👔👕

Every Drip Has Advantages & Disadvantages!

How you dress dictates opening quotes, seller patience, and unique tactical superpowers. Pick the build that fits your style!

🪖
Market Soldier (Street Veteran):
🟢 Advantage: Lowest opening quote (+10%), patience decays 25% slower, and Fake Call bluff is 100% believed.
🔴 Disadvantage: Show Cash fails on luxury goods, and high-end sellers start with lower patience.
🎧
IJGB / Diaspora Returnee:
🟢 Advantage: 95% Walkaway Callback rate (panicked sellers chase you), and Show Cash has +45% closing power.
🔴 Disadvantage: Starting prices are inflated by +80% ("Maga tax"), and lowballing triggers double patience loss.
🎒
Student on a Budget:
🟢 Advantage: Sweet Talk restores +38 patience, and audacious lowballs cost 45% less patience (lowball forgiveness).
🔴 Disadvantage: Show Cash is laughed off on luxury goods, and sellers ignore your walkouts (only 45% callback).
👔
Island Banker (9-to-5er):
🟢 Advantage: Sellers slash prices 15% faster per round due to lunch-hour urgency, with +20% Show Cash transfer credibility.
🔴 Disadvantage: Opening quote has a +45% salary tax, and starts with -15 base patience (rushed lunch clock).
