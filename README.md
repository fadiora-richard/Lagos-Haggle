# 🇳🇬 Lagos Market: The Art of the Haggle

An authentic negotiation and street survival game inspired by life in Lagos markets (Yaba, Balogun, Computer Village, Mile 12, and Alaba International) and the EA FC Transfer Market negotiation system.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 1. 🧺 Saturday Errand (Dynamic Campaign / Budget Mode)
* **Procedural Missions**: Choose between 3 errand difficulties:
  * 🥪 **Quick Errand (2 Items)**: Fast provisions rush.
  * 🧺 **Standard Saturday (3 Items)**: Classic family weekend market run.
  * 🚚 **Mega Owanbe Hustle (4 Items)**: High-stakes party procurement across Lagos!
* **Dynamic Scenarios & Calculated Budgets**:
  * Story scenarios vary procedurally (*Saturday Owanbe Party Emergency*, *NEPA Blackout & Survival Crisis*, *Uncle London Returnee Rush*, *Weekend Family Provisions Run*).
  * Mama calculates a realistic, tight cash budget based on wholesale floors. If you don't haggle, you run out of money and face bankruptcy!
* **The Reward**: Every single Naira saved from the budget is **your personal pocket money**.
* **Mid-Trip Lagos Road Hazards**:
  * Unpredictable transit events between markets: *Third Mainland Bridge Go-Slow* (Gala & ice-cold Lacasera), *Ojuelegba Pickpocket Commotion*, *Mama's Surprise Phone Call*, and *Danfo Radiator Overheat*.
* **Mama's Return Home Inspection**:
  * Full authenticity inspection breakdown: **★ Certified Original** vs **Grade Replica / Thrift**.
  * Mama inspects each item and delivers witty commentary with an overall grade from **S-Tier (Pride of the Ancestors)** down to **F-Tier (Disowned from the Family)**.

## ⚡ Consequence System & Street Tactical Moves (1x per Stall)
1. **🍯 Sweet Talk**: Flatter the seller to recover up to $+35\%$ patience and soften their mood.
2. **🔍 Inspect Fault (With Consequences!)**:
   * **If item is Grade Replica / Thrift**: $85\%$ chance to spot loose threads or flaws, forcing a $30\%$ price drop.
   * **If item is Certified Original**: The seller gets deeply insulted (*"God forbid! You dey find fault inside genuine original?!"*), triggering an immediate **price hike** and slashing $-25\%$ patience!
3. **📞 Fake Call**: Pretend a rival stall at the front is selling cheaper. Seller panics and undercuts to keep your trade!
4. **💵 Show Cash**: Slap cold hard Naira notes onto the counter. Triggers an instant deal close if within reasonable range of the floor.

## 🎨 2D Sprites & The Drip System (Pros & Cons)
Every outfit build comes with distinct tactical advantages and street disadvantages:
* **Market Soldier (Street Veteran)** 🪖:
  * 🟢 **Advantage**: Lowest opening quote ($+10\%$), patience decays $25\%$ slower, ₦200 Agbero toll, and Fake Call bluff is $100\%$ believed.
  * 🔴 **Disadvantage**: Show Cash tactic fails on luxury items (crumpled notes mocked), and high-end sellers start skeptical.
* **IJGB / Diaspora Returnee** 🎧:
  * 🟢 **Advantage**: $95\%$ Walkaway Callback rate (panicked sellers chase you), and Show Cash has $+45\%$ instant-closing power.
  * 🔴 **Disadvantage**: Starting quotes inflated by $+80\%$ ("Maga tax"), ₦1,500 Agbero toll, and lowballing triggers double patience drain.
* **Student on a Budget** 🎒:
  * 🟢 **Advantage**: Sweet Talk restores $+38$ patience, lowballs cost $45\%$ less patience (lowball forgiveness), modest $+20\%$ opening quote.
  * 🔴 **Disadvantage**: Show Cash is laughed off on luxury items, and sellers ignore your walkouts (only $45\%$ callback).
* **Island Banker (9-to-5er)** 👔:
  * 🟢 **Advantage**: Sellers slash prices $15\%$ faster per round due to lunch-hour urgency, and bank transfers have $+20\%$ Show Cash credibility.
  * 🔴 **Disadvantage**: $+45\%$ salary tax markup, starts with $-15$ base patience (rushed clock), and ₦800 Agbero toll.

## 🌪️ Lagos Market Chaos Events
Unpredictable real-life Lagos interruptions that trigger during bargaining:
* **The Competing Customer (FOMO)**: A rival buyer rushes in waving cash to snatch the item. Match their bid, call their bluff, or walk away.
* **Sudden Rainstorm**: Dark clouds gather and thunder claps! Sellers frantically throw nylon tarps over their goods and offer emergency clearance price drops.
* **Area Boy / Agbero Salute**: A local boy salutes you (*"Chairman, boys dey loyal o!"*). Pay ₦500 to gain street respect and seller patience, or ignore him.

## 🏆 EA FC Transfer Market Style Grading
Once a deal is agreed upon, your deal efficiency is calculated:

$$\text{Efficiency} = \frac{P_{ask} - P_{agreed}}{P_{ask} - P_{floor}}$$

* **A+ (≥ 88%)**: **Senior Street Veteran / Oga Boss** (Shaved off almost all excess margin)
* **A (74% - 87%)**: **Sharp Lagosian / Master Haggler** (Near wholesale purchase)
* **B+ (55% - 73%)**: **Market Wise** (Great street deal)
* **B (38% - 54%)**: **Average Bargainer** (Solid effort)
* **C (20% - 37%)**: **Ajebutter / Soft Life** (Overpaid)
* **F (< 20%)**: **Pure Mugu / JJC (Journey Just Coming)** (Accepted opening quote or folded immediately)
